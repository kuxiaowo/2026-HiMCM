Background 
Nepal is endowed with rich and varied biodiversity. Altitudinal variances in 
short distance give Nepal's biogeography variety that range from lush moist forests 
and sparse alpine deserts to luxurious g rasslands in lowland Tarai. The 
mountainous country also shelters some of the world's most rare animals. 
Sagarmatha (Mt. Everest) National Park and Chitwan National Park with typical 
natural, cultural and landscape characteristics were listed as World Heri tage sites 
in 1979 and 1984, respectively. 
Nepal has established a good network of protected areas. There are twelve 
national parks, one wildlife reserve, one hunting reserve, six conservation areas 
and thirteen buffer zones in and around national parks an d reserves in Nepal 
(Figure 1). Within the coverage of protected areas in the country, conservation area 
covers highest part which is 44.82 % of total protected areas where as national park 
occupies 34.3 %, buffer zone area occupies 15.52 and hunting reser ve and wildlife 
reserve cover 3.85 and 0.51 % respectively (Table 1). Not only the protected areas 
were established, Government of Nepal has intensified the protection measures 
too.

A Geographical Information System (GIS) is a system of hardware, software 
and procedures to facilitate the management, manipulation, analysis, modeling,

representation and display of geo‐referenced data to solve complex problems 
regarding planning and management of resources. A GIS plays an important role as 
a tool for environmental management, with the current greater concern for 
sustainable use of resources, and conservation and monitoring of biodiversity.  In 
this direction, the GIS based database on exis ting area coverage within and around 
the protected areas by different administrative tiers has to be identified and created 
on thematic map and tabular form of database.  
 
Objectives: 
The prime objective of this study is to generate the GIS based database o n 
existing area coverage of protected areas by local level. However specific 
objectives were; 
 Support DNPWC in GIS Database management 
 Prepare local level database and maps on area coverage by all PAs of Nepal 
(including BZ) 
 
Methodology: 
 Collected and comp iled the existing GIS related protected area coverage 
data base from the department as well as relevant sources  (DoS and 
MoFAGA). 
 Verified with field offices by consultation to field staffs as per required and 
updated with central level information of Nepal Gazette records. 
 Updated on final database and prepared report with fact figure on 
distribution of protected areas of Nepal by Province, District, 
Nagarpalika/Gaunpalika and Ward Level. 
Output: 
 Province, District, Nagarpalika/Gaunpalika and Ward Level Ar ea Coverage 
Database on Protected Areas of Nepal with maps showing each 
administrative level boundary for all PAs including BZ.
